You can comfortably walk on new concrete after 24 to 48 hours. For vehicles and parking on driveways or shed slabs, we recommend waiting 7 to 10 days for standard passenger cars, allowing the slab to reach high curing strength under Perth temperature conditions.
Exposed aggregate is created by washing away surface concrete to expose round textured river pebbles, making it ideal for vehicular driveways with maximum grip. Honed concrete is mechanically ground flat to a silky smooth matte plane—ideal for alfresco areas and around pools because it feels pleasant under bare feet while retaining slip resistance.
